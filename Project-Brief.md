# **My Project Brief** 



##### **Part 1: The Question**



Which areas in Ibadan North LGA have steep slopes, and where are they located?



##### **Part 2: Why It Matters**



Steep slopes can affect where buildings, roads, and other development can be safely located. Knowing where these areas are can help with better land-use planning and environmental management.



##### **Part 3: The Data I Need**



Ibadan North LGA boundary

Elevation/DEM data

##### **Part 5: What I Would Build**



I would create a GIS map showing the different slope areas in Ibadan North LGA. The map will make it easy to see where steep slopes are located and how they are spread across the area, providing useful information for land-use planning and development.
